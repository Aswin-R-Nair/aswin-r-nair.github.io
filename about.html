CV / Resume

Hello, I'm Aswin R Nair! I am currently a Research Assistnant at the Raman Research Institute in Bengaluru. I work on creating simulations of radiation transport in the Interstellar Medium. I graduated with a master's degree in astrophysics from the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Mohali in 2025.

In the past I have worked on various numerical simulations that deal with diverse astrophysical phenomena, where I use both analytical and computational methods to model and understand these systems. My masters thesis was on "Numerical Simulation of Accretion onto Compact Objects with Hard Surface" and you can read more about it here.

Right now I am trying to transition into the field of AI safety. I was part of the BlueDot Impact Technical AI safety June 2026 cohort. I am working on a personal project that involves understanding what we can learn from activation directions extracted from different evaluation datasets.
